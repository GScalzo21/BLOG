<h1 align="center">🛡️ From Detective to Defender: How One Conversation Sparked My Cybersecurity Career</h1>


![cyber-security-concept-shield-with-keyhole-icon-vector](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1934d229-cfb4-4977-b209-da4b2439d3e1)


I didn’t always know I wanted to work in cybersecurity. My journey began when I signed up for the police academy back in 2015, eventually working patrol — responding to crimes both in progress and after the fact, interviewing victims, and following leads wherever they took me. I was later selected to join the Domestic Violence Unit, where I served as a detective for five of my eleven years in law enforcement, working over thousands of cases.

One day during a shift, a close friend mentioned he was studying for the CompTIA Security+ exam. At first, I was genuinely curious — especially when he told me his wife was already in the field and described cybersecurity as “the digital world of policing.” That line stuck with me. The deeper we talked, the more I saw the overlap: investigations, pattern recognition, protecting people, and staying ahead of threats — just in a different landscape.

I realized many of the same instincts applied. In law enforcement, especially in investigations or crimes in progress, I had to think like the suspect — anticipate their next move, notice inconsistencies, and dig into small details others might overlook. That same mindset translates directly into threat hunting and incident response in cybersecurity.

That conversation planted a seed. I started exploring the field and began with the fundamentals, eventually studying for and passing Security+. I was hooked. From there, I enrolled in a cybersecurity degree program, began stacking certifications, and fully dove into Blue Team work.

Today, I’m working toward my **Bachelor’s in Cybersecurity (graduating December 2025)** and interning with **Log(N) Pacific**, where I support:

- Threat hunting and alert triage in **Microsoft Defender XDR**
- Writing **KQL queries** to detect suspicious behavior
- **Remediating STIG findings** and hardening Windows endpoints
- Mapping detections to the **MITRE ATT&CK framework**

I’ve earned certifications including **CompTIA Security+**, **Microsoft Azure Fundamentals**, and the **TCM Security SOC Analyst (hands-on)** — and I continue to grow through hands-on labs, personal projects, and writing about what I’m learning (many of which you’ll find here in this blog).

This space is where I document the journey — from the street to the SOC. If you’re transitioning careers, building detection skills, or just passionate about defense, I hope you find something here that helps or inspires you.

**Let’s hunt some threats.**
